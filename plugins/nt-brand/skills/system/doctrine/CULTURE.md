# NoTambourine's culture: how we work

These are the defaults we use to decide what to do on an engagement.

## Write the work down

Decisions travel through pull requests, issues, preview links, runbooks, and
focused threads. Put important context where another person can find and
challenge it. A call can move a decision forward; write down what it decided.

## Make the call and roll forward

Make a recommendation and act on it instead of asking and waiting. Two forms:

- UNODIR: "Doing XYZ unless otherwise directed."
- Veto: "Doing XYZ. Tell me if I should do ABC instead."

"Did XYZ" beats "should I do ABC or XYZ?" even when some calls need rework,
because the wait for an answer costs more than rolling forward. Ask for help
when a constraint is real, not for permission to start. This autonomy works
because the hiring bar, scope, and feedback are explicit.

When someone asks for more analysis, another review, or a longer test, state the
cost before starting: the days, the people, and the work that waits behind it.
The person asking then decides with that cost in view. Name stalled work in the
channel where the decision lives.

## Fit the client's operating system

We join the client's process instead of installing our own. Ceremonies and
approval gates stay only when they catch a concrete risk or improve a decision.
Talk to a person instead of sending a report nobody reads. When the client has
no process, the senior engineers carry one. Measure progress in value delivered,
not points estimated.

## Hire for capability, not headcount

Each engineer owns delivery and uses AI throughout it; we don't build pods
around a coordinator. Leaders stay active individual contributors who can
inspect the work directly and price a tradeoff from evidence. We hire to add
capability, never to fill a management layer.

## Make mistakes usable

Postmortems, playbooks, and corrections are part of the work. Record recurring
patterns where the next engineer will find them. An error that is named and
examined costs less the next time. We reward honest records of what failed and
what changed.
