# Ecommerce site speed

[[ecommerce]] [[site-speed]] [[lighthouse]]

An ecommerce retailer, measured with Lighthouse before and after a site-speed
release that went live on 30 September 2026.

- Pages are about 4x lighter: total mobile page weight across the audited pages
  fell from 206 MB to 48 MB.
- Mobile Largest Contentful Paint fell from 8.4 s to about 5 s.
- Cumulative Layout Shift is zero on every audited page.
- Cart LCP fell from 9.2 s to 6.2 s on mobile and from 2.0 s to 1.1 s on
  desktop.

Copy line:
`Pages load 4x lighter, and mobile load time fell from 8.4 to 5 seconds.`
