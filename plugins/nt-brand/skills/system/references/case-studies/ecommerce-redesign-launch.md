# Ecommerce redesign launch

[[ecommerce]] [[redesign]] [[platform-migration]] [[ga4]]

An ecommerce retailer moved to a new platform with a redesigned site on 15
September 2026. Read: the first 17 days (15 September to 1 October) against the
same weekdays averaged over the four weeks before launch. Sources: the order
feed and GA4.

- Orders rose 42% and revenue 40% over the baseline.
- Orders ran 8% ahead of the same weekdays of 2025, after trailing them by 27%
  the week before launch.
- Mobile, three quarters of traffic: sessions rose 10%, engagement rate went
  from 59.0% to 70.4%, add-to-cart rate from 5.9% to 8.1% (+38%), and conversion
  rate from 1.87% to 2.19% (+17%).

Cite the mobile rates as the redesign's effect. Paid shopping spend roughly
doubled from the second week, so order and channel volume carry that spend too.
Quote percentages only; the order and revenue counts identify the client.

Copy line:
`After our most recent redesign launched, mobile add-to-cart rose 38% and conversion 17%.`
