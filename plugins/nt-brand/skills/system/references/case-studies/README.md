## White-label rules

- Describe the client by sector and shape ("an ecommerce retailer"), never by
  name, initials, nickname, domain, logo, screenshot, or a detail that
  identifies them.
- Keep the name out of everything we write about the case: commits, PRs, issues,
  filenames, tags, notes, threads, and chat. A client's own channel may use
  their name; nothing that leaves it does.
- State the metric, the comparison, the measurement window, and the tool.
- Add a case only with the client's permission to share the numbers.
